The global university, Arunachal Pradesh hereby informs all prospective students, parents, and the general public that the University does not authorize any external agency, consultancy, agent, or individual to collect fees, process applications, or grant admission on its behalf.

Admissions are conducted only through the official University website and the University’s Admission/Information Office.

The University will not be responsible for any loss of money, documents, or opportunities arising from dealing with unauthorized persons or agencies.

ABOUT THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY

Welcome To The Global University

At The Global University, we are proud to be a beacon of academic excellence, innovation, and community engagement. University was widely recognized as one of the prominent educational institutions of the state of Arunachal Pradesh. Since 2023, University is a leading group known amongst the top education institutions in India. Our university has been at the forefront of shaping minds, fostering creativity, and empowering individuals to make a positive impact on the world.
The Global University, Established with the aim of delivering excellent education and actively participating in social responsibility, our University strives to make a positive impact.The Act envisions the University to found, conserve, oversee, and enhance campuses.
University started to expand progressively in all the fields of education namely, LAW , Medical, Science, Engineering, Management, Design and many more.
The University is dedicated to providing students with high-quality, values-driven, and future-focused programs developed by academic experts and professionals. The program strives to nurture individuals into professionals and entrepreneurs.

Aim and Objectives

The University has established with the Object of “ University will teach job oriented courses and small scale industry based courses so that the youth will become independent and self-employed too.

We will start courses in airplane, helicopter maintenance, repairing, and skill development & vocational courses in various fields as it is the dream of our present Prime Minister too.

We will have industry relations & placement department for the help of students in their career.

We have long term strategy and vision for the Students community in future. The great philosopher Sri Vivekananda said that Education will reach at the door of the students no matter where they live, following this principle we have decided to reach to the rural.

History

At the heart of a university are its dedicated faculty members, who are experts in their respective fields and play a pivotal role in guiding students toward academic excellence. Research is a cornerstone of the university, with faculty and students collaborating on projects that address complex global challenges, contribute to scientific breakthroughs, and inspire new ways of thinking.

Furthermore, a university is not just a place of learning; it is a vibrant community where students form lifelong friendships, engage in extracurricular activities, and participate in a rich tapestry of experiences that help shape them as well-rounded individuals. Alumni of universities often go on to make significant contributions in their chosen careers, becoming leaders, innovators, and agents of positive change in their communities and the world at large. In essence, a university is a source of knowledge, a crucible of ideas, and a catalyst for personal and societal growth. It embodies the pursuit of truth, the spirit of inquiry, and the promise of a brighter tomorrow for all who pass through its gates

Vission & Mission

Vission :
To transform into an internationally recognized university with independence and a multi-disciplinary approach, dedicated to fostering learner-centric education, cultivating trails for holistic individual development, and promoting innovation and scholarship to influence both practice and communities.

Mission :
1. Delivering inclusive and high-quality higher education to every segment of society.
2. Establishing research platforms at both national and global levels.
3. Persistent focus on utilizing technology for Teaching progress and effective governance.
4. Empowering rural communities to gain and enhance skill sets for sustainable development, prioritizing accessibility and inclusivity.

Values

1.Respect for Knowledge & Integrity - Respecting expertise and knowledge for its own sake and behaving ethically in all interactions at all levels.

2.Creativity & Innovation - The core intellectual aim of our university education is to foster creativity & innovation in the students and faculty, calling for a greater integration of knowledge among all domains.

3.Merit & Inclusiveness - within the given policy framework of education, all the students, irrespective of descent and prospects, are on a completely equal footing. We are open to everyone and include everyone who shares our quest for knowledge and commitment to pursuing truth, creativity and innovation, and the other virtues of higher education.

4.Freedom to Ideate – We are a space in which people associated with us are free to pursue all ideas without any discrimination. We are a centre of independent thought.

5.Lifelong Learning & Excellence - Encouraging enthusiastic, independent thinkers and learners striving for personal growth while maintaining high levels of performance leading to the achievement of academic and career goals.

A Journey

THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY is a venerable institution with a rich history that spans centuries. Its story is one of evolution, resilience, and commitment to education and knowledge.

Founding and Early Years

THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY was founded in 2024 with a mission to provide accessible and high-quality education to the community. It started as a modest institution with just a few academic programs and a handful of dedicated faculty members. Over the years, it grew steadily and gained a reputation for academic excellence.

Periods of Growth

Throughout its history, THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY has witnessed several transformative phases. These pivotal moments have shaped the university's identity and contributed to its standing as a respected institution.

Modern Era

In recent decades, THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY has embraced the digital age and adapted to the changing educational landscape. It has expanded its academic offerings, embraced innovative teaching methods, and forged international partnerships to offer students a global perspective.

Achievements and Contributions

THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY takes immense pride in its alumni who have achieved greatness in various fields. Graduates have made significant contributions to society, business, arts, and academia, leaving a lasting impact on the world.

Community Engagement

The university remains deeply committed to community engagement and social responsibility. It actively participates in outreach programs, research initiatives, and community development efforts, reinforcing its role as a pillar of the local and global community.

Conclusion

As it looks back on its illustrious history, THE GLOBAL UNIVERSITY remains dedicated to its core values of knowledge, innovation, and inclusivity. It continues to evolve, inspire, and prepare the next generation of leaders, while preserving its legacy as an institution that has played a pivotal role in shaping minds and societies.
